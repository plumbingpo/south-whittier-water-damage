Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Across South Whittier, South Whittier Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Professional extraction also addresses moisture that you cannot see or reach with consumer tools. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Program (913) 210-6477 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly

When to Call for Water Extraction in South Whittier

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
